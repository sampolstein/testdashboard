Competitive Positioning Notes

Bedrock Robotics is the #1 competitive threat. Same Waymo pedigree narrative, same retrofit approach, but with 3.8x AIM's funding and a $1.75B valuation. Key differentiator: AIM has defense contracts + Earthbrain/Komatsu partnership + mining deployments. Bedrock is construction-only for now.

Caterpillar is the 800-lb gorilla entering the space with OEM-integrated autonomy. Their CES push signals construction autonomy is now mainstream. AIM should position as the brand-agnostic retrofit alternative to Cat's walled garden.

Built Robotics has pivoted to solar pile-driving, effectively exiting the direct earthmoving competition. Reduced threat.

SafeAI was acquired by Pronto and is now focused on mining haulage. Differentiated enough to be moderate threat only in mining segment.
